What we store Что мы храним Was wir speichern Ce que nous stockons

When you connect the extension to our backend we keep a small profile so the clan features work. The full list of fields:

  • Wall ID + username — to identify you across sessions.
  • Discord ID + Discord username — to show your tag and proxy your Discord avatar (so you don't load it from Discord's CDN on every page).
  • Role (member / clan moderator / admin) — for permissions.
  • Preferred language (en/ru/de/fr) — to localise responses.
  • Custom avatar — only if you uploaded one. Stored as WebP, max 256×256.
  • Clan chat messages, templates, moderation requests, notifications — content you've actively created.
  • Session tokens — hashed, used for "stay logged in".
